01 — ACADEMIC WORK

5G · RESOURCE SCHEDULING · ENERGY EFFICIENCY

Energy-Efficient Resource Scheduling in 5G

An academic project exploring how scheduling decisions can reduce energy use while preserving the quality of resource allocation in 5G systems.

Role · Research framing and analysis

02 — QUESTION

How can scheduling become more energy-aware?

5G systems need to allocate resources while responding to changing workloads and network conditions. The project focused on the tension between using resources effectively and reducing unnecessary energy consumption.

03 — APPROACH

Treat energy as part of the scheduling decision.

01Model the trade-off

Frame resource allocation as a decision that considers both service needs and energy cost.

02Compare scheduling behavior

Evaluate how different scheduling choices affect utilization and energy efficiency.

03Look for practical balance

The goal is not simply to minimize energy, but to avoid efficiency gains that make the system less useful.

04 — TAKEAWAY

The interesting part was the trade-off, not a single number.

This project made me more interested in optimization problems where engineering choices have competing objectives. It also reinforced a pattern I want to continue in graduate study: connect implementation with a clearer understanding of why a system behaves the way it does.
